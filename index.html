02 · The bottleneck

The step that decides your margin — usually done under pressure.

Marker making and nesting are where fabric is actually won or lost. Most teams still do it by hand, against a deadline, with no real way to compare options.

The old way

A marker is built by hand, piece by piece, against the clock. There's rarely time to try a second layout, so the first one ships — even when it wastes fabric. Efficiency ends up being whatever the marker maker's afternoon allowed, not what was actually possible. Consumption is estimated, then disputed later.

→
With Gelian

Marka builds the marker from a description, in minutes. Ultranest nests it for the best efficiency in the industry and shows you the number before you commit. Compare real options instead of a single guess — on every style, every time, without adding a single hour to the schedule.
