About Gelian

The most advanced fabric cost management software in the market.

Gelian Fashion offers web-based, human intuition driven AI-powered marker making and nesting software for the apparel industry — the most advanced fabric cost management software in the market.

Our Story

Built by someone who lived inside the problem.

At Gelian, we specialize in marker making — the process of arranging every pattern piece for a garment, or a batch of garments across sizes, onto a layout called the "marker" that gets placed on top of the fabric before cutting.

Think of it like a jigsaw puzzle. You're fitting every piece — sleeves, collars, fronts, backs, pockets — usually across multiple sizes and multiple garments, onto a single length of fabric with as little wasted space as possible. The tighter the fit, the less fabric is wasted, and that flows straight through the business: fabric utilization to fabric consumption, fabric consumption to fabric cost, fabric cost to the cost of the garment itself. It used to be done entirely by hand on paper; today it's done partly by hand and partly in CAD software.

Our founder is a specialist in this space. He spent over a decade as a fabric consumption planner inside one of Asia's largest apparel production houses — building markers, planning material, and chasing efficiency for some of the biggest fashion brands in the world.

In performing his daily tasks, he used every so called industry leading marker-making, nesting and related software systems currently available in the world and still found to have too many manual work, long waits to arrive at a workable layout & solutions , limitations in functionalities in practical use cases, fabric utilization efficiency plateaued at 70–80%.

Once accounted for limitations built into those software systems and the manual interventions required complete the tasks, overall fabric cost saving practically achieved by such software systems seemed negligible, leaving room for visible improvements in performance, as those systems lack the human intuition of how the designing, marker making, fabric cutting are handled in real production environment.

Studying these limitations up close and delivering practical fixes to address them for over decade led him to architect his own software from the ground up, addressing almost all the practical bottlenecks come across in fabric utilization optimization in real garment production set ups that even today's so called industry-leading CAD systems still unable to address.

Our product — Seamline Suite entirely automate all the critical functions of marker making process start to end — aiding apparel manufacturers arrange and nest pattern pieces onto fabric efficiently — cutting waste, lead time and production cost.

Compared to all the completive software offerings in this space, we have something unique that no existing software could offer and this is the secret recipe for our product achieving 85%-90% fabric utilization. That is — our founder's human intuition of over 20,000 hours of practical problem solving at marker making, production planning and fabric cost management at the most advanced garment production set up available in the Asian region.

Our founder has combine this human intuition led expertise with advanced mathematics and artificial intelligence to forge the most advanced software in marker making to manage fabric costs in garment design to production. This made Seamline Suite the most advanced fabric management software in the market in terms of functionality and useability.

Vision

To be who apparel manufacturers turn to for marker making and nesting — closing the gap between the 70–80% efficiency most cutting rooms accept and the 85–90% that's actually achievable.

Mission

To build AI-powered marker-making and nesting software that runs entirely in the browser — so the most time- and fabric-consuming step in production takes minutes, not a full shift.

Our approach
01
One bottleneck, done right
We don't try to be every tool in the workflow. Marker making and nesting are where fabric is actually won or lost, so that's where we've put the depth — built entirely for the browser, without the standalone installs and version mismatches of traditional CAD software.
02
AI where it matters
Marka builds a marker from a description in minutes; Ultranest nests it for the best efficiency in the industry and shows you the number before you commit. Your team spends its time on the decisions that need a person, not the repeatable ones.
03
Data you can act on
Efficiency and consumption data surface as they are generated, in interfaces built for the decision at hand — not buried in a report you pull at the end of the week.

Every percentage point of marker efficiency is fabric that was never cut and never wasted — margin protected before a single yard reaches the table.
