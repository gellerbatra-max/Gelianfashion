About Gelian Fashion

Gelian (Pvt) Ltd. builds web-based CAD software for the apparel industry, with AI integrated into the design, marker making and cut planning workflow.

Vision

To be the platform apparel manufacturers turn to for design, product development and cut planning — web-based, AI-native, and built around the decisions that make a style profitable.

Mission

We build CAD software that runs entirely in the browser and puts AI to work on repeatable tasks, so design and production teams spend their time on the decisions that shape efficiency, cost and speed to market.

Our approach

No installs, no silos

We are the only CAD solution in the industry built entirely for the browser — pattern design, marker making, nesting and PLM in one platform, without the standalone installs and version mismatches that come with traditional CAD software.

AI in every workflow

AI agents are integrated across the apparel workflow, making repeatable, error-prone steps autonomous — generating a pattern from a sketch and spec, building a marker from a conversation, calculating and entering consumptions. Your team spends its time on the decisions that need a person.

Data you can act on

Efficiency, consumption and cut-plan data surface as they're generated, in interfaces built for the decision at hand — not buried in a report you pull at the end of the week.
