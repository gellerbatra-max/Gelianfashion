Pattern design
Seamline Pattern Design Studio
AI assistant: Sutra

Chat with Sutra, provide a measurement spec and a sketch of the garment, and get a pattern generated directly in the PDS interface — no manual drafting required. The full manual toolset stays available for hand-built work.

Marker making
Seamline Marker Maker
AI assistant: Marka

Chat with Marka and describe the marker you need — Marka builds it. The manual marker-making workflow has also been reimagined, so teams who work by hand get a faster path too.

Product data
Seamline PLM
AI assistant: Bomma

Every style's data lives in one place. Ask Bomma for a style's BOM or consumption status. Integrated with Ultranest, it receives consumption automatically into the style's BOM once nesting runs. Give it a customer's buy quantity and Ultranest generates and enters sales-order-level consumptions too.

Fabric utilization
Seamline Roll Management
AI assistant: Spoola

Spoola reads each fabric roll's length and width and recommends which roll suits which marker — lifting efficiency and utilization through width segregation across your roll inventory.
