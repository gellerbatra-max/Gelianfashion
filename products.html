Solutions

Two solutions. One bottleneck, solved.

Fabric is commonly the largest single cost in a garment. Most cutting rooms run 70–80% marker efficiency; Gelian is built to get you to 85–90%. Marka, our AI marker-building assistant, builds the marker; Ultranest, our AI nesting engine, nests it — that gap is where the two solutions do their work.

Solution 01

Seamline Marker Maker

Describe the marker you need in plain language and Marka builds it — the walk from pattern to marker drops from hours to minutes. The manual workflow has been reimagined too, for teams who work by hand.
